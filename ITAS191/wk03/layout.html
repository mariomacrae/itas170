About this page

In this digital age, a computer is a necessity for work, communication, school, and play. Back in the 80s, computers mostly came in desktop form and it didn't mater as much what brand you bought. But walk into Staples or Best Buy in 2026, and you'll see 10 types of computers for 5 kinds of users with 8 different configuration. With so many options, it can be hard to know what you need for your setup. This guide is here to help you decode the most common computer lingo and how to use it to your advantage.

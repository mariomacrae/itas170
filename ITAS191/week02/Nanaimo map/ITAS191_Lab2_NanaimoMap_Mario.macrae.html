Details About Places

Colliery Dam Park

I walk through this park every day. It's a beautiful area close to VIU- perfect for going for a walk or run after class. There are two manmade lakes in the park that are excellent swimming spots.

Long Lake

Just off the highway, Long Lake is the perfect destination for summer fun. The lake has several rafts to swim up to, a dock for fishing, and a sandy beach.

Waterfront/Maffeo Sutton Park

Maffeo Sutton Park is located in the centre of town. There is an extensive seawall that goes through the park with a beautiful view of the ocean. In the summer, many outdoor concerts are held at the park's stage. The park is also a popular place for food trucks.

Westwood Lake

Close to the university, Westwood Lake is a popular recreation site. There are walking trails and a sandy beach. In the summer, the lake warms up nicely for swimming. It is advisable to come early and secure a parking spot- the lot fills up fast!

Mount Benson Regional Park

Mount Benson Regional Park is a great place to go if you like hiking. The trails are surrounded by lush forest, and there are beautiful panoramic views at the top. I have never been but hope to visit someday.
